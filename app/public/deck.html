02

The problem

Performance marketing runs on distrust, delay, and a 30% tax.

SLOW

Paid last

Affiliate networks settle on Net-30 to Net-60 terms and hold your money behind payout minimums.

EXPENSIVE

20–30% taken

The whole fee stack exists to referee two parties who can't verify each other.

UNACCOUNTABLE

Attribution disputes

Who drove the sale is decided after the fact by whoever owns the dashboard. Businesses pay blind.

03

The solution

Escrowed USDC + an autonomous agent turns the referee into software.

Sub-second settlement and USDC-denominated gas on Arc make it possible to pay per verified result — instantly, at near-zero cost, down to sub-cent revenue-share. The contract holds the money; the agent releases it. Neither side trusts the other.

  • Budgets lock in escrow before anyone promotes — no working on a promise.
  • Per-result, instant payouts — a tasker earns the second a referral converts, not at month-end.
  • Unspent budget returns automatically, permissionlessly, even if Vane disappears.
  • No wallet, no seed phrase, no jargon — Circle Wallets creates accounts invisibly.
04

How it works

Three steps, and nobody has to trust anybody.

01 · LOCKED

A business funds a campaign

USDC moves into the VaneEscrow contract. Nobody — not Vane, not the business — can move it. It's released only against verified results.

02 · PROVEN

The referral is sealed onchain

A tasker takes the campaign and gets a link. The referral is recorded before the conversion, so attribution can't be rewritten after the fact.

03 · PAID

The falcon settles or refuses

Verified results pay out in ~1s. Fraud is refused with a written reason — which keeps the pot intact for everyone honest.
